The service

Arova is a review-request and customer-communication platform for local service businesses such as plumbing, HVAC, and electrical contractors. Business customers use Arova to send text messages to their own customers requesting feedback after a completed job.

SMS messaging program

Program name: Arova review requests.

Program description: After a completed job, Arova sends a text message on a business's behalf to that business's customer, asking the customer to leave a review or share feedback. Messages include a link to leave the review.

How customers opt in: End customers provide their mobile number and agree to receive a follow-up text at the point of service — during or after the job, collected by the business verbally or in writing. Only customers who provided their number and agreed to be contacted receive messages.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies. Typically a customer receives one message per completed job, and occasionally a single follow-up.

Cost: Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Arova does not charge end customers to receive messages.

To opt out, reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts.

For help, reply HELP, or email arovahq@gmail.com.
