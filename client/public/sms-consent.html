SMS Verbal Consent Process

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Arova provides SMS messaging software for local trade and home-service businesses. This page documents the verbal consent process used before an end customer may receive SMS messages through Arova.

How consent is obtained

Customers opt in verbally during an in-person or telephone service interaction with the local trade or home-service business providing their service. Consent is obtained before any SMS message is sent.

The employee or technician reads the following disclosure to the customer:

“Would you like to receive service-related text messages from [Arova], including follow-up messages requesting feedback or a review about your completed service? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Privacy Policy: https://app.getarova.co/privacy.html. Terms of Service: https://app.getarova.co/terms.html. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Do you agree to receive these text messages?”

Recording consent

The customer must affirmatively answer “yes” before consent is recorded. If the customer declines or does not affirmatively consent, SMS consent is not recorded, no SMS messages are sent, and the customer may still receive the underlying service without agreeing to SMS messaging.

After the customer verbally agrees, the employee records the customer's consent status in Arova. Arova only permits SMS messaging to customers whose prior consent has been recorded. The first outbound SMS is sent only after verbal consent has been obtained.

Opt-out and help

Customers may reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages. Customers may reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Privacy

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

See the Arova Privacy Policy and Arova Terms & Conditions for additional information.
