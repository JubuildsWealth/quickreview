Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Arova provides review-request and customer-communication software to local service businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Arova is operated by Julyan S Barajas as a sole proprietorship ("Arova," "we," "us").

Who this policy is for

Two groups interact with Arova. Business customers are the trade businesses that sign up to use the software. End customers are the customers of those businesses, who may receive a text message requesting feedback about a completed job. This policy covers both.

Information we collect

  • From business customers: name, business name, email address, phone number, and account and billing details needed to run the service.
  • About end customers: the name and mobile phone number that a business customer provides so Arova can send a review request on the business's behalf.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as log and device data used to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To send review-request text messages on behalf of a business to that business's own customers.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Arova service.
  • To process payments and manage accounts.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate about the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent abuse.
We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers collected for messaging are never sold or shared for marketing, and consent to receive messages is never shared with third parties.

How information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us operate Arova — for example, our messaging provider (Twilio), payment processor (Stripe), and hosting and database providers. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Text messaging

End customers receive messages only after they provide their mobile number and expressly consent verbally during an in-person or telephone service interaction with the business providing their service. Consent is obtained before any SMS message is sent. Every message identifies the business and includes opt-out instructions. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies — typically one message per completed job, and occasionally a single follow-up.

Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information collected as part of the SMS program is used only to deliver the messages you agreed to receive and to operate the service.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to promptly address any issues.

Your choices

  • End customers can opt out of messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
  • Business customers can close their account at any time.

Children

Arova is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
